Abigail’s Driving School has been serving Virginia drivers since 2012. Beginning in October 2026, we are returning to Behind-the-Wheel instruction, while continuing to offer convenient online driver education and training programs.

Our online programs include Virginia-approved Driver Education for teens and adults in accordance with Virginia requirements, as well as Driver Improvement, the Driver’s Manual Course for individuals who have failed the knowledge test three times, and Reckless/Aggressive Driver Education Program (RADEP) courses.

With the return of Behind-the-Wheel instruction, Abigail’s Driving School offers both in-person driving instruction and flexible online education to meet the needs of new drivers, individuals completing DMV or court requirements, and those looking to improve their driving skills.

#### Our owner

Abigail’s Driving School is owned and operated by Mohamed G. Abbamin, MPA. He has more than a decade of experience in driver education and is the author of the Virginia-Focused Driver Improvement Program.
