<section class="faq-group" aria-labelledby="choosing">
<h2 id="choosing">Choosing a Course</h2>

<details open>
<summary>Which course do I need?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<ul>
<li><strong>Driver Improvement Clinic — 8 hours:</strong> for a DMV or court requirement, voluntary improvement, or a possible insurance benefit.</li>
<li><strong>3X Fail Driver’s Manual Course — 8 hours:</strong> for eligible students who have failed the DMV knowledge exam three times. A Driver Improvement Clinic does not meet this requirement.</li>
<li><strong>RADEP — 12 hours or 4 hours:</strong> for a reckless/aggressive driving referral. Check your referral before choosing a package.</li>
<li><strong>Virginia Online Driver Education — 30 hours:</strong> coursework for new drivers. This does not replace behind-the-wheel training or all other licensing requirements.</li>
</ul><p>Compare tuition below, then use the registration links for your selected program. If you are unsure, <a href="#faq-help">ask us before paying</a>.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the difference between the 12-hour and 4-hour RADEP options?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<p>The <strong>12-hour package</strong> includes an 8-hour Driver Improvement Clinic and the 4-hour RADEP course. The <strong>4-hour option</strong> is for students who have already completed the required Driver Improvement component.</p><p>Confirm the required program, accepted format, prior-course documentation, and deadline with the court or referring agency before registering.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can a student under 18 take the 3X Fail Driver’s Manual Course?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<p>Eligibility depends on prior driver education. Students under 18 who finished classroom driver education <strong>before</strong> their third knowledge-test failure must provide that completion certificate to the school before enrolling. Other students under 18 may need the classroom driver education component instead.</p><p>Review the <a href="https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/training/three-exam-failures">DMV’s three-exam-failure requirements</a> with us before paying. Failing the road test requires a different type of training.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Are online courses accepted for my DMV or court requirement?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<p>Acceptance depends on the specific program and your referral. Check the course’s approval and delivery format against your DMV notice or court order. For a court requirement, confirm that the court accepts the selected online course.</p><p>Completing a course does not guarantee ticket dismissal, a reduced fine, or a particular case outcome. See <a href="https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/improvement">Virginia DMV’s clinic guidance</a>.</p>
</div>
</details>
</section>

<section class="faq-group" aria-labelledby="completion">
<h2 id="completion">Completion &amp; Certificates</h2>

<details>
<summary>How is my completion reported, and when do I receive my certificate?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<p><strong>Driver Improvement Clinic:</strong> the clinic must report completion electronically to DMV within 24 hours. Keep your completion certificate and follow any separate court-submission instructions.</p><p><strong>Driver’s Manual Course:</strong> provide your completion certificate to DMV before retesting. For Driver Education and RADEP, follow the issuing provider’s certificate and submission instructions.</p><p>Contact us to confirm certificate delivery and timing for your course, particularly if you have a deadline. <a href="https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/improvement/di-reason">DMV reporting guidance</a> · <a href="https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/training/three-exam-failures">Driver’s Manual completion guidance</a></p>
</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How long do I have to finish, and what happens if I fail a test?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<p>Your court or DMV deadline and your course-access period are separate. Check both before enrolling, and leave time for any exam, retake, certificate delivery, and submission.</p><p>Ask us to confirm the final-exam format, passing requirements, retake limits, waiting periods, and any fees for your selected course. The rules can differ by program.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will I receive safe driving points?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<p>Eligibility depends on your age, attendance reason, and driving record. Court-directed students need court documentation authorizing points. Insurance-discount attendance does not earn safe driving points.</p><p>Tell us your attendance reason before starting. Review <a href="https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/improvement/di-reason">DMV’s point eligibility rules</a> for your situation.</p>
</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will completing a course reduce my insurance premium?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">
<p>A discount is not automatic. Ask your insurer whether you qualify, which course it accepts, and what proof it needs before enrolling. Court-directed attendance and point-related eligibility have separate rules.</p><p>See <a href="https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/licenses-ids/improvement">Virginia DMV’s insurance guidance</a>.</p>
</div>
</details>
</section>
